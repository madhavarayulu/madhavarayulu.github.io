## The Wilderness of Enterprise ERP

If you have ever worked on an Oracle Fusion Finance implementation, you know the feeling.

You are deep in the configuration of Subledger Accounting rules, or trying to trace why an AP invoice failed validation, and you realize the official documentation is... sparse. Or perhaps you find a workaround that took you three hours to figure out, but it is buried in a forum post from years ago.

## Why "Cairn"?

In the mountains, a cairn is a stack of stones built to mark a trail. It is not a grand monument. It is a humble, practical collection of stones left by those who walked the path before you, so that those who come after do not get lost.

That is the purpose of this blog.

## What to Expect

Here, you will find:
- **Troubleshooting:** Exact steps to resolve specific Oracle Fusion errors.
- **Configurations:** Deep dives into how specific modules should be set up.
- **Concepts:** The "why" behind the "how" in financial system design.

Every post is a stone placed on the trail. I hope it helps you on your journey.
