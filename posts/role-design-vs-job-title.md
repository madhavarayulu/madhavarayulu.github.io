---
title: Role design vs job title
date: 2026-05-05
tags: Security, process
---

A recurring source of friction: building Fusion roles that mirror job titles instead of actual tasks.

Job titles change. Tasks are more stable.

A better approach:

- List the concrete actions a person must perform (create invoice, approve payment, run period close, etc.).
- Build roles around those actions.
- Assign roles in combination rather than creating one giant role per title.

This produces smaller, clearer roles, fewer security conflicts, and less rework when someone changes position but keeps similar responsibilities.
