title: The hold nobody applied
date: 2026-01-22

An AP invoice sat on hold for eleven days. The hold said *price mismatch*, the buyer swore the price was right, and everyone had better things to do than argue with a checkbox.

The invoice was fine. The purchase order was fine. The mismatch was three weeks old, on a different line entirely — a receipt booked against line 2 instead of line 3, quietly, by someone rushing on a Friday. The matching engine doesn't hold grudges; it holds arithmetic. It was comparing the right numbers, just not the numbers anyone was looking at.

The lesson generalizes: **when a hold makes no sense, stop auditing the invoice and start auditing the history.** Match review first, receipts second, tolerances last — the hold names the symptom, never the scene of the accident.

Eleven days of an unpaid supplier for a thirty-minute correction. Someone should have left a marker about this. So — marker left.
