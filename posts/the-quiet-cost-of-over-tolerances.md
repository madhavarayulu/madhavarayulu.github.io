---
title: The quiet cost of over-tolerances
date: 2026-03-14
tags: AP, matching, process
---

It is easy to widen matching tolerances so that invoices stop hitting holds. It feels like progress.

It is often just deferred work.

Wide tolerances let small quantity and price differences flow through without review. Over time those differences accumulate in inventory valuation, accrual balances, and supplier relationships. The holds were never the real problem — the underlying process gaps were.

A better default:

- Keep tolerances tight enough that genuine exceptions still surface.
- Treat repeated holds as process signals, not noise.
- Fix the root (PO accuracy, receiving discipline, price updates) instead of silencing the alarm.

The goal is not fewer holds. The goal is fewer *legitimate* reasons for holds.
