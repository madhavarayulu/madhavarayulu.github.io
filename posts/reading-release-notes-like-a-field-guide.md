---
title: Reading release notes like a field guide
date: 2026-02-08
tags: releases
---

Every quarter Oracle ships a release, and every quarter most teams experience it as a hundred-page document that is somehow both mandatory and optional reading. It doesn't have to be read like a document. It reads like a field guide: you are not looking for everything, you are looking for what moved in *your* terrain.

Three passes, twenty minutes:

- **Your modules only.** Search for the ones you own — GL, AP, AR, FA — and skip the rest without guilt.
- **Red getters.** Most features arrive switched off; the red pages in the feature list are the ones that change behavior without asking. Those are yours.
- **One test per catch.** Anything that touches your flow gets a sandbox check before the update hits the real trail. One test, not a project.

The teams surprised by quarterly updates were never surprised by the notes. They just never walked the ground before the weather changed.
