---
title: The accrual that never cleared
date: 2026-04-21
tags: AP, accruals, gotchas
---

An accrual journal kept appearing every period. No one could find the original uninvoiced receipt that was supposed to clear it.

The receipt existed. It had been corrected (quantity adjusted to zero) months earlier, but the correction had been done in a way that did not reverse the accrual. The system still saw an open quantity waiting for an invoice.

**When accruals refuse to clear:**

- Look for corrected or cancelled receipts, not just open ones.
- Check whether the receipt was adjusted after the accrual had already been generated.
- Verify the accrual reversal setup for the period.

Accruals are only as clean as the receiving and correction process that feeds them.
