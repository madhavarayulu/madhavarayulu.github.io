## Context

I've spent years working with Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials. In that time, I've solved the same problems multiple times, forgotten solutions I'd found before, and learned from consultants who generously shared their notes. This blog is my attempt to leave a trail.

## Why Cairn

A cairn is a stack of stones left by travelers. Each stone is small. Together, they mark a path for whoever comes next. No single stone matters on its own. But the cairn does.

That's what I want this site to be. Each post is one stone. A configuration note. A troubleshooting walkthrough. An observation about how a module actually behaves in the field. Over time, the cairn grows.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Configurations** — setup steps, navigation paths, and parameter notes for Fusion Finance modules.
- **Troubleshooting** — symptom, root cause, resolution, prevention. Real problems and real fixes.
- **Concepts** — how Fusion Finance actually works, explained in plain language.
- **Observations** — things I've noticed about consulting, implementation, and the gap between documentation and reality.

## What You Won't Find

- Oracle proprietary screenshots or content.
- Marketing fluff.
- AI-generated filler.
- A promise to post daily.

I'll post when I have something worth leaving behind. That's the only cadence that matters.
