I've solved the same problems multiple times. I've forgotten solutions I found before. I've learned from consultants who shared their notes freely. Cairn is my attempt to pay that forward.

## What Cairn Is

A personal blog. A knowledge trail. A stack of stones left for whoever comes next.

## What Cairn Is Not

- Not an official Oracle site.
- Not a marketing page.
- Not a promise to post daily.

I write when I have something worth leaving behind.
